# Phase 3: city geometry and urban accuracy (plav-town, 4 × 4 km)

Generated by `pipeline/fetch_evidence.py` + `pipeline/verify_city.py` on 2026-11-21. Every number below is in
`pipeline/cache/plav-town/verify-city.json`; per-building results are in `public/data/plav-town/verify.json` and on the
map with `#verify`.

**Bottom line.** No footprint, street or public space was moved, reshaped, added or deleted in this phase. Every
building now carries an evidence tier, measured against sources produced independently of the map data. 1,620 of
3,526 buildings (46 %) are verified or confirmed. 1,365 are shown to exist, but their outline is unconfirmed. 520 have
no independent evidence either way, and 552 of those lie in the cloud-covered south-west. 21 are disputed. None of
the possible corrections met the two-source rule after a check by eye (see "Corrections considered").

## Why the earlier zero-error audit was not enough

`audit_geo.py` only had 10 m Sentinel-2, which cannot see a single house. Its zero errors meant that nothing was
detectably wrong at 10 m. It did not show that anything was right. This phase adds sources that resolve single buildings.

blocked from the sandbox.

## How the 1 m imagery was used

* **Registration.** As delivered (product L1), the image sits **38.03 m north and 1.93 m east** of OSM, Microsoft and
  Sentinel-2. The offset varies smoothly across the town (north 34.43 to 44.78 m, east -5.5 to 9.9 m).
  It was measured in 206 overlapping 448 m windows and interpolated smoothly. Each window was left out in turn and predicted from
  the others: the error was 0.95 m (median) and 1.89 m (90th percentile). Because the registration ties the image to the
  footprint layer as a whole, the image **cannot judge the absolute position of the town**. Sentinel-2 does that
  (`GEO_ACCURACY.md`: no shift beyond about 2 m). The image judges each building against its neighbours.
* **Clouds.** Cumulus covers the south-west of the pass. Cloud and shadow were masked and grown by 60 m, which hides
  24.88 % of the image. No imagery evidence is used there: 552 buildings, mainly in Nadgrad, Vardište,
  Završ, Brezojevice and Đurička rijeka.
* **Roof test.** For each building, the test compares NDVI in a 1.5–5 m ring outside the footprint with NDVI inside it,
  in units of pixel noise. It was calibrated on OSM footprints (positives) against the same shapes moved onto open
  ground (null). A roof counts as visible above 6.54, a threshold that 5 % of open ground also passes. Detection rates for OSM houses:
  25-60 m² 24.14 % (open ground 0.0 %), 60-120 m² 49.74 % (open ground 7.14 %), 120-250 m² 70.13 % (open ground 3.77 %), 250- m² 57.89 % (open ground 14.81 %).
  So a visible roof is strong evidence. A roof that is not visible proves nothing.
* **Position test.** A ±6 m search finds where the roof best fits the footprint. On the gold set (275 buildings where OSM
  and Microsoft agree *and* the roof is visible), that residual is 2.1 m (median) and 4.11 m (90th percentile). This is the noise of the test
  itself (noisy 1 m pixels, eaves, registration), so **4.11 m is the tolerance**: the test cannot see a misplacement smaller than that.
* **Orientation.** A rotation search on the image gave a median of 8° even on the gold set, which is noise. It was dropped.
  Orientation is checked only where OSM and Microsoft both have an outline.

## Footprints: OSM against Microsoft

Of 764 OSM buildings, 484 have a Microsoft outline with IoU ≥ 0.5. Centroid distance: 1.19 m (median) and 2.99 m (90th percentile).
Orientation difference: 1.74° (median) and 8.2° (90th percentile). 41 buildings (5.58 %) differ by more than 10°. These are listed in
`verify-city.json`, but no source can say which outline is right. Neither layer shows a systematic offset (median dx and dy are 0.0 m).

## Streets

Each car road (60 m or longer, not service) was given a cross-profile of the 1 m image every 4 m, with roofs masked.
Only 33 of 306 segments show a clear road line. Gravel shoulders, trees and the noise of the 1 m pixels hide the rest. On
those 33, the image's road line lies 1.0 m (median) and 4.0 m (90th percentile) from the OSM centreline. 84.85 % are within 3 m. 2 segments are ≥ 5 m off
(Bogajićka and an unnamed residential road south-east of the centre). This is within the registration error, so neither was moved.
Carriageway widths stay as estimated in `CITY_ESTIMATES.md`. The image is too noisy to measure them.

## Spacing between buildings

Nearest-neighbour gap between buildings of 25 m² or more. Shown layer: 0.56 m (10th percentile), 3.93 m (median), 15.4 m (90th percentile), 6.36 %
touching. Raw Microsoft: 0.35 m, 3.56 m, 14.51 m, 9.1 % touching. The density and rhythm of the town match the
independent producer. The shown layer touches less often because OSM separates terraced buildings that Microsoft merges or packs together.

## Corrections considered and not made

* **21 disputed footprints.** An inspection by eye (`chips-disputed.jpg`) shows that several stand 2–4 m from a small
  bright object, probably a shed or metal roof, and two lie near cloud. The rule needs two sources to agree on "no
  building", and the inspection shows that cannot be relied on for objects this small. **None were removed.** They are red in `#verify`.
* **Buildings that may be missing.** GlobalBuildingAtlas carries 237 Microsoft *2024* footprints that are in neither Microsoft 2026 nor
  OSM. The 1 m image shows a roof-like signal at 51 of them. An inspection by eye (`chips-gba.jpg`) shows that about half are clear
  roofs. The others are yards or road junctions, which also look non-green. **None were added.** The 51 are shown as magenta
  squares in `#verify` as a mapping list. Adding them would need a person to check them, or an OSM mapper to trace them.
* **Earlier removals.** Of the 108 footprints removed earlier, 6 tiny ones and 6 on-street ones show a roof-like
  signal. On a street, asphalt gives the same signal, so they stay removed.
* **Positions and orientations.** No building shows a residual beyond the tolerance of the test that a second
  source confirms, so nothing was moved.

## Limits

* The 1 m image is from one pass, on 2022-08-31. Anything built later is "unconfirmed". The south-west quarter is under cloud.
* The roof test misses 30–75 % of real houses, depending on size. Most of the "present" and "unconfirmed" tiers are a limit of the evidence, not an error in the map.
* The image cannot check the absolute position of the town: it is registered to the map layer. Sentinel-2 checks that, at about 2 m.
* Heights and roofs are unchanged and still estimated (rules v2).

```
python pipeline/fetch.py plav-town            # Overture etc. (cache)

python pipeline/audit_geo.py plav-town        # only if audit_geo.json is missing

python pipeline/verify_city.py plav-town      # ~10 min
```
