## Lake

* **Shoreline:** the OSM outline is kept as mapped. Area **1.974 km²** (published: 2 km²), perimeter 8.6 km,
  extent 1.57 × 2.18 km.
* **Sentinel-2 check:** in summer 2025 only **1.215 km²** of it is open water; **0.742 km²** is covered by
  vegetation (reeds / sedge / floating plants — Sentinel-2 cannot tell which). Open water outside the OSM outline:
  1,600 m² (a few mixed pixels), so the outline contains all of the open water. The open-water edge lies a median
  124 m (N), 138 m (E), 117 m (S) and 37 m (W) inside the mapped shore. See `docs/water/shoreline-s2.jpg`.
* **What the model shows:** the whole OSM polygon is water; over the vegetated part a low reed canopy is drawn per
  10 m Sentinel-2 cell, its density from NDVI, its colour 40 % from the cell's own Sentinel-2 colour. Reed height
  (0.35–1.1 m canopy, tufts to ~2.5 m) is **stylised**, not measured. Water under the belt is darker and olive;
  the canopy is kept low along every mapped pier and boardwalk so they stay visible.
* **Water level:** **904.5 m** — GLO-30's flattened water value (it is the 5th–50th percentile inside the lake).
  Ground 20 m outside the shore: median 907.2 m, 5th percentile 904.9 m, so the shore stands above the water all
  round. The published value is **907 m**. The 2.5 m difference is a datum / DEM-editing difference (GLO-30 heights
  are EGM2008; national maps use the Trieste datum); the model keeps 904.5 m so the water and the Phase 1 terrain
  agree. Seasonal level changes are not modelled (no source found).
* **Depth:** not modelled (no open bathymetry). The bed is carved 2 m under the surface only so the terrain never
  shows through.

## Rivers

* **Courses:** OSM centrelines (drawn in flow direction; flipped automatically if a merge reverses one) and OSM
  river areas. Mean mapped width: Lim 27.9 m, Ljuča 22.5 m, Vruja 22.3 m, Grnčar 25.9 m.
* **Levelled surfaces (new):** a station every 10 m along each river: the 15th percentile of GLO-30 within 12 m,
  then the closest profile that never rises downstream (least-squares isotonic fit, so one low DEM pixel cannot
  drag the rest of the river down), smoothed. The **Lim starts exactly at the lake level** (904.5 m) and
  falls to 805.9 m at the edge of the model (14.9 km, 6.6 m/km; nearly flat for the first 2.8 km through Plav).
  The **Ljuča ends exactly at the lake level** (916.2 → 904.5 m over 14.3 km). Before, river surfaces followed the
  local minimum of the DEM and could step up or down. Figure: `docs/water/river-profiles.png`.
* **Banks:** inside a mapped river area the bed is cut 1.2 m under the levelled surface (both in the 8 m city grid
  and in the Phase 1 30/60/180 m levels); nothing outside the river polygons changes. Changed terrain cells: 889 of
  252k in the city grid (all within ±5 m), 480 of 161k in L1; 21 L1 cells are cut more than 5 m (where GLO-30
  reads trees or bridge decks over a narrow river).
* **Culverts removed:** 14 mapped stretches with OSM `layer=-1` (247 m in total, streams piped under streets and
  yards in town) were drawn as open water; they are now left out. One building near one of them sits 0.5 m higher
  because the ground there is no longer cut down.
* **Lake crossing removed:** OSM routes the Ljuča line through the lake to the Lim outlet; the part inside the lake
  is no longer drawn as a ribbon. Intermittent streams (`intermittent=yes`) are drawn 40 % narrower.

## Rendering (all tiers keep their previous cost class)

* **Water v2 material:** Fresnel reflection with two scrolling noise normals; lighter shallows near the mapped bank,
  a thin wet rim along it; on rivers the ripples are stretched and move along the profile's flow direction.
* **Planar lake reflection** (medium and high only): a mirrored camera with an oblique near plane at the lake level
  renders terrain, far terrain and sky only (no buildings, trees or water) into a target at 0.33 (medium) / 0.5
  (high) of the screen, only on frames that are drawn anyway and only while the lake is in view. Low uses the sky
  colour instead.
* **Reed belt:** one mesh (≈17k triangles) + instanced tufts at the open-water edge (medium) and inside the belt
  (high). Low draws the canopy only.

## Remaining uncertainties

Status: conditionally approved 2026-11-21; water frozen at 18e5cfd. Full register with evidence and what would settle
each point: `docs/water/UNCERTAINTIES.md` (lake elevation, shoreline vegetation, raft construction first).

1. **Water level:** 904.5 m (GLO-30) vs 907 m published; relative heights are consistent, absolute value is not.
2. **Seasonal shoreline:** Sentinel-2 is a summer 2025 snapshot; spring high water and the winter ice are not modelled.
3. **Vegetation type and height:** reed vs sedge vs floating plants (water lilies) and real reed height are unknown;
   only the cover is measured.
4. **Lake depth / bathymetry:** no open source; not modelled.
5. **Splavovi construction** (floating or on piles, railings, roofs) and its current state: not documented; drawn as
   a plain deck.
6. **Boardwalk details** (railings, widths): generic 2.2 m timber walkway.
7. **River widths** come from OSM river areas; Sentinel-2 at 10 m cannot resolve a 20–30 m river well enough to
   check them.
8. **Bridges:** structure type, piers and parapets unknown; Zioci is the only mapped bridge over the Lim inside the model.
9. **Culverts:** only stretches tagged `layer=-1` in OSM are hidden; unmapped culverts are still drawn open.
10. **Beyond ±9 km** (lower Lim, Gusinje springs) rivers are coloured from WorldCover only.
